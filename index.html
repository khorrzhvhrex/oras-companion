Quick Reference

DexNav Cheatsheet

⌄
Core Rule

Higher Search Level improves your chances of better IVs, Egg Moves, Hidden Abilities, held items, higher levels, and Shinies.

Search Level

0 — Level shown

2 — First Move shown

3 — Ability and held item shown

5 — Potential shown

Search Level can continue increasing up to 999.

Potential Stars

★ — at least 1 perfect IV

★★ — at least 2 perfect IVs

★★★ — at least 3 perfect IVs

Additional perfect IVs can still occur randomly.

What the ! Means

! by Move — Egg Move

! by Ability — Hidden Ability

! by Level — boosted level

Approaching

Use the Circle Pad gently and sneak toward the shaking grass or visible tail.

Moving too aggressively can scare the Pokémon away.

Repels are useful because normal encounters can interrupt a chain.

Chaining

Catch target CONTINUES
Defeat target CONTINUES
Failed DexNav search SAFE

Chain Bonuses

Every 5th chained encounter receives improved bonuses. Encounters 50 and 100 receive additional Shiny boosts.

Expanded DexNav

After resolving the Primal Kyogre crisis, additional non-Hoenn Pokémon begin appearing through DexNav on earlier routes.

Standard Methods

Level — reach the required level.

Stone — use the required Evolution Stone.

Trade — trade the Pokémon.

Trade + Item — trade while holding the required item.

Friendship — level up with high friendship.
